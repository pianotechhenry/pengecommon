# The Commons Series — claims for adversarial verification

Each claim below is held in the project record as resting on recollection or a
single weak source. Evidence files: `verification/evidence/V01.md` … `V50.md`.

## Tier 1: Book One, the arrival scene

- **V01** The 1554 Egyptians Act (1&2 Ph.&M. c.4) and the 1562 Act (5 Eliz. c.20): does either exempt Egyptians who leave the company and settle, and does either name service with an honest householder as the way out? Read the statute texts.
- **V02** Any dated record of Egyptians/Gypsies dealing in horses in England before 1600?
- **V03** Barnet's fair, chartered 1588: were horses traded there by the 1590s?
- **V04** Smithfield's Friday horse market (FitzStephen, c.1173): confirm, and confirm it was still London's horse market in 1589.
- **V05** Croydon's fair(s) in 1589: dates, and whether horses were sold.
- **V06** First recorded dates of "horse-courser", "horse-dealer" and "coper" (horse sense).
- **V07** Counter-Armada, spring 1589: where the Queen's ships lay in March–April (Medway/Chatham, the Downs, Dover). Dates for Drake and Norris at Dover, passing Rye, and sailing from Plymouth, in Old Style and New Style. Could a watcher on Sheppey see ships gathering?
- **V08** Sale of Horses Act (31 Eliz. c.12): royal assent 29 March 1589 and in force 18 April 1589? What the toll-book had to record, from the text.
- **V09** Erection of Cottages Act (31 Eliz. c.7): the four-acre rule and its commencement.
- **V10** Earliest British evidence of Romani living-wagons (claimed c.1850s).
- **V11** Privy Council letters of 1 March 1569 ordering the arrest of Egyptians in Surrey (Loseley MSS).
- **V12** York 1596 (106 condemned, nine hanged): is there any primary source? Durham 1592, a parish-register hanging "for being Egyptians".
- **V13** Hale, Pleas of the Crown: exactly what Hale says about executions under the Egyptians Acts.
- **V14** Rogationtide: do Lambeth churchwardens' accounts record a perambulation reaching the Vicar's Oak in the 1580s? Any evidence that Battersea walked Penge's bounds?
- **V15** Thou/you in 1560–1600: what Walker (2007) and other corpus studies find. "You" the default and "thou" marked, or a fuller system?
- **V16** The age-fourteen threshold in the Egyptians statutes and the 1572 Vagabonds Act: confirm from the statute texts.

## Tier 2: Book Two's opening and the existing draft (1684–1689)

- **V17** Was an eight o'clock curfew bell rung at St George's, Beckenham?
- **V18** "Ma'am": first recorded, and would a bound boy in 1684 say it to a collier's wife? (Alternatives: mistress, dame.)
- **V19** First recorded dates of "get lost" (as a dismissal), "see you later", "golden boy", and "erm"/"um" as a written hesitation.
- **V20** The parish constable c.1689: an annual office by rotation? Did he answer to the vestry or to the justices? Anything for Battersea.
- **V21** 1686 Act (1 Jac. II c.17): was forty days' residence counted from a written notice? Was settlement by indenture introduced in 1691?
- **V22** James II's final flight: 23 December 1688, Old Style?
- **V23** Judge Jeffreys died in the Tower on 18 April 1689?
- **V24** Was fish legally required on Wednesdays, Fridays and Saturdays in 1689?
- **V25** Signature literacy among labourers in the 1680s (Cressy 1980): the figure.
- **V26** Romani words (for the consultant): sar "how" vs so "what"; dai (British) vs dej (Vlax); jertis; British spelling of panch. Use Sampson (1926), Smart & Crofton (1875), Matras.
- **V27** "Rowan" as an English given name before the twentieth century.
- **V28** Charcoal: the clamp burning season (April–September or to October); the dates of London's switch to sea coal; coal rising from 20s. to 32s. a chaldron in 1689; cylinder charcoal for gunpowder from the 1780s.
- **V29** THE SMOKE CODE (a deeper item). What do practitioner sources say smoke colour and density mean during an earth-clamp burn, stage by stage? Start with Evelyn, Sylva (1664), on EEBO-TCP, and any other 17th-century account. Then later English practitioner accounts. Keep earth clamps apart from steel ring kilns, and say which method and period each source covers. Is "thin blue smoke = burning true" supported?
- **V30** "Forest" in ordinary late-17th-century speech: still mainly a royal hunting district, or also simply a large wood?

## Tier 3: the record, the walking tour and memory

- **V31** Any primary source that the 1827 Penge enclosure hearings were held at the Crooked Billet? Any source for the inn existing in 1601 earlier than Abbott (2002)?
- **V32** The Vicar's Oak site today: the 1988 "Boundaries Gate" memorial; was a replacement oak planted (2018?); what is physically there.
- **V33** The Vicar's Oak's end: all dated evidence (Survey of London: "probably soon after 1647"; Aubrey; J.B. Wilson).
- **V34** John Morgan, promoter of the 1827 Penge Inclosure Act: who was he? The award dated 13 February 1837 (Bromley Historic Collections ref. 258).
- **V35** Do Saxton (1575) or Norden (1590s) show the Penge/Norwood woods? Does any 1607 map name "Pensgreene"?
- **V36** Norden, Surveyor's Dialogue (1607): confirm the surveyor-versus-farmer argument and locate the passages (EEBO-TCP).
- **V37** Holm oak: when was it introduced to Britain? Is the Betts Park "veteran holm oak" a relic or a planting?
- **V38** Bossema (1979) on jays caching and planting acorns.
- **V39** Sir Walter St John's school, Battersea: founding date.
- **V40** "Penge among the most heavily bombed areas of London": Bomb Census or LCC bomb-damage evidence.
- **V41** Penge in the Metropolitan Board of Works area to 1889; County of London 1889; Kent 1900.
- **V42** Bus route 157 first reached Crystal Palace in 1959?
- **V43** The Croydon Canal company's unpaid land debt surfacing in the 1827 enclosure accounting; Edward Richards Adams senior opposing enclosure then taking an allotment. Any source for either?
- **V44** Jan Assmann's threshold for communicative memory: exactly what he wrote, and where.
- **V45** Lethieullier conveyance of Kent House, April 1709 (TNA Discovery).
- **V46** The August 1797 arrests of about 30 at Norwood: who made them (the Union Hall public office, 1792?).
- **V47** Peg-making as a Romani trade: earliest English evidence.
- **V48** The 1/7th Royal Welch Fusiliers: was it the Merioneth and Montgomeryshire battalion, with D Company at Machynlleth?
- **V49** Margaret Finch: the earliest sources for her age at death (108 or 109) and her birthplace (which Sutton: Kent or Surrey?).
- **V50** The 1703 Great Storm: Defoe's Kent and Surrey tree counts (about 17,000 in Kent; Evelyn's 2,000 in Surrey). Any correspondent near Norwood?
